# Playtesting Rule or Drool

> **For the owner, not for testers.** Send everything below the line, as it is or pasted into a
> form. It works for anyone with a browser, so a playtest does not have to wait for the Play
> closed test; the closed test's testers get the same brief. What to do with the files that come
> back is in `twa/STORE.md` (*Your developer account*): put them in `playtests/`, which git
> ignores, and run `npm run playtests`. Keep the deck the same while people play. The report
> replays only the runs dealt from its own deck, and the menu's footer names the deck in use.
> The per-version notes in `twa/STORE.md` say where each question below comes from.

---

## What you're testing

Rule or Drool is a satirical card game about running a made-up country. Each card is a decision:
drag it left or right. What your choices do builds up over a run, and not all of it is shown.

## Where to play

- In any browser, on a phone or a computer: https://rcjlabs.github.io/Rule-or-Drool/
- On a phone you can add it to your home screen, and it then works like an app, offline too.
- If you were invited to the Google Play test, the Play app is the same game.

## Before your first run

1. Open **Settings** and turn on **Keep a record of my runs**. It starts with your next run and
   stays on your device until you send it.
2. That is all the setup. Please don't look up how the game works, and don't ask how the votes
   work: part of what is being tested is what people work out from the screen alone.

## While you play

- Play the way you would if nobody were watching. There is no right way to play, and a run that
  falls apart early is as useful as one that goes the distance.
- Ten runs or more is the most useful, over a week or two, but any number helps.
- When something breaks, looks wrong or confuses you, jot it down there and then, with the
  version from the bottom of the menu. It looks like "v0.82.0 · deck c8agx015".
- If the game shows "Something went wrong", its last line is the version and the error: send
  that line. If it says "Not saved", use **Move my progress** from the notice to keep your
  profile safe.
- If Settings says the record is full, send it, delete it, and carry on.

## When you're done

1. Open **Settings** and press **Send my record**. It opens your share menu, or saves a file
   where there isn't one. Send it to whoever invited you.

   It is plain text, so you can open it first and see what it holds: the game's version, each
   run's code, the side you took on each card, how long each card and each side's preview was in
   front of you, and the game's numbers, some of them hidden while you play, before and after each
   choice. Nothing about you, your device, your settings or when you played.
2. Answer the questions below. A line each is plenty. Skip any that don't apply.

## Questions

**How it went**

1. About how many runs did you play, and on what: an Android phone, an iPhone, a computer?
2. Did the game feel too easy, about right, or too hard? Did any run come close to falling
   apart?
3. How did your last run end? Did the end screen, or its chart of how the run went, tell you
   anything you didn't already know?
4. By your tenth run or so, was the end screen still telling you something new, or had you
   started skipping it?

**Choices**

5. Think of a card where you took the side you knew was worse for the country. Why did you take
   it?
6. Was there a card where one side was so obviously right that it wasn't really a choice?
   Roughly what was it about?
7. When a vote came up, how did you decide what to do?
8. When did you first think of your rival as a threat: before a vote, at one, or never? Did their
   moves feel like someone playing against you, or like nagging?

**If it happened to you**

9. - If you lost the office: what did you do while you were out of it, and did the end of that
     run tell you what had happened?
   - If you did away with elections: did anything tell you the risk that came with it, and did
     you do anything about it?
   - If a side of a card was ever marked as one that would end your rule: did it change what you
     chose?

**Beyond one run**

10. Did a clue in the codex, or one of the week's contracts, send you after something? Did you
    get it?
11. Did you take over the country a previous run left behind? Why, or why not?
12. What do you remember the picture of the country showing, under the card and between eras?

**Anything else**

13. Anything confusing, unfair or broken; anything that read like a real party, person or
    country; anything that stopped being funny by the third time.
