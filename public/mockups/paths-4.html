Round four

Both paths are chosen: Stream for Drool, Holo for Rule. These are iterations rather than alternatives — each one is a modifier on the look that won, so what differs between them is the decision and nothing else. The originals are at the top of each section, unchanged.

Drool — Stream, three intensities

These are not three ways of doing Stream, they are Stream at three volumes — which is the question the phase actually has to answer. A competent run spends 8.2% of its cards in the first Decay stage and 0.1% in the third, so whichever of these is the baseline is the one nearly everybody sees. Quiet is a candidate for stage 1, Loud for stage 3, and Overtaken is the argument that stage 3 should change the layout rather than the volume.

Rule — Holo, two palettes and a structure

Cool is the version where legibility wins: the card gets a real surface and the glow comes off, which is what it would have to look like anyway under the readable setting. Warm asks whether the projection has to be cyan — it is the most generic science-fiction choice available, and gold reads closer to the bright future than to a console. Depth keeps the palette and changes the structure: three planes rather than one pane.

Cards are the deck's own: d05_unpaid_police, d09_ghosts, d10_l_hall, d06_exodus on the way down; a07_complacency, a08_succession_bench, a06_newcomers, a05_maintenance on the way up.
