Where Drool came from

Round two's Stream, unchanged, so the five below are judged against it rather than against a memory of it.

Drool — the stream, and who is paying for it

Stream's idea was that the room is talking while you read. These five ask the next question: who is paying for the room. An advertiser, a shopping channel, the viewers themselves, three things at once, or nobody — the country buffers while the advertisement plays in full quality.

Rule — a country that got to the future

A change of claim, not just of palette. The document looks said a working country leaves good paperwork; this says a working country builds the thing — tube transport, machines that still have physical buttons, public services that are simply pleasant. Bright and slightly absurd on purpose, because a utopia that takes itself entirely seriously is the other kind of story.

Cards are the deck's own: d05_unpaid_police, d04_queue, d08_archive, d09_ghosts, d02_lights on the way down; a09_long_bond, a05_maintenance, a06_newcomers, a04_visitors, a07_complacency on the way up. The Rule looks need two webfonts and the cost lines say so.
