Two paths, ten looks

Candidate treatments for the Decay and Ascent looks, drawn over real cards from the deck. The point of the exercise: the game is named after two looks a player mostly never sees. Five for Drool, five for Rule — one of each gets built.

  • full Decay is 0.1% of a competent run's cards
  • full Ascent is 1.9%
  • stage 3 needs |drift| ≥ 55, which is 1.6% of cards
  • a cynical run sees full Decay on 5.3%
  • round two is at paths-2.html
  • round three is at paths-3.html

Drool — the path down

The shipped look is a 2005 web page: yellow, Comic Sans, hot pink, a tilt. It is ugly rather than designed to be ugly, which is a different thing. Each of these is a specific idea about what kind of decline this is.

Rule — the path up

The shipped look is an annual report: cool blue-grey, a serif, wide spacing. It is pleasant and says nothing in particular. Each of these is a claim about what a country that is working actually looks like from the inside.

Cards are real: d05_unpaid_police, d02_lights, d04_queue, d01_payroll, d03_two_maps on the way down; a01_surplus, a03_poaching, a02_expectations, a04_visitors, a01_surplus on the way up. Meter values and the Decay slang for the blocs are the game's own.
