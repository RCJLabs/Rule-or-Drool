What came through

Unchanged from round one, reprinted here so the new ones are judged against them rather than from memory.

Drool — the screen that is selling you something

Channel said the state arrives as a broadcast; Feed said it arrives as a post. Both are really the same claim — that you are not being governed, you are being programmed to. These five take that further apart: who is talking, how many of them at once, and whether there is a card left at all.

Rule — the document it becomes

Record said a working country produces paperwork that will outlast the person who signed it. Five different pieces of paper, each with its own typography and its own idea of what a decision is: a resolution, a notice, a line item, a cable, a clause.

Cards are the deck's own: d09_ghosts, d07_barter, d08_archive, d10_l_hall, d06_exodus on the way down; a05_maintenance, a06_newcomers, a09_long_bond, a04_visitors, a08_succession_bench on the way up.
